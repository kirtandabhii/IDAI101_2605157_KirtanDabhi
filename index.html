ScamShield AI will never ask you for an OTP, PIN, card number, password or ID document. It is a chatbot. It is not a bank, the police, or a government body, and it has no authority to act for you. For financial fraud in India, calling 1930 is the first move, before anything else.

Under the hood

How it was built

Dialogflow ES, no webhook. Nothing you type is sent to a server of mine, because there isn't one. The intent classifier runs at a threshold of 0.4 rather than the default 0.3, since 41 intents in one narrow domain will otherwise produce confident wrong answers, and a wrong scam verdict is worse than admitting uncertainty.

Fuzzy matching is on for 18 of the 20 entities, because people typing during a fraud make spelling errors. It is deliberately off for yes/no and scam/safe, where an approximate match would be a real bug. Suggestion chips appear on every reply, so someone who cannot phrase the question can still get an answer by tapping.
